MONOPOLY ONLINE - HOW TO PLAY WITH FRIENDS FAR AWAY
=====================================================

THE BIG IDEA
Your computer is the "game referee". Your friends connect to YOU using a
5-letter room code. Everybody sees the same board and only clicks their own
player. Nothing to install, no accounts.

STEP 1 - PUT THE GAME ON THE INTERNET (one time only)
Your friends need a web address to open the game. A free site called Netlify
can give you one in about a minute.

  1. Open your web browser and go to:   app.netlify.com/drop
  2. On your computer, open the folder:  Desktop > Claude > monopoly-online
  3. Drag the WHOLE "monopoly-online" folder onto the Netlify page.
  4. Wait a few seconds. Netlify shows you a web address that looks like
     something-random.netlify.app
  5. Write that address down. That is your game's web address!

  Tip: Netlify may ask you to make a free account so the address stays up
  forever. Without an account it can disappear after a while. If it
  disappears, just drag the folder in again to get a new address.

STEP 2 - START A GAME (you, the host)
  1. Open your game's web address.
  2. Type your name and pick a token.
  3. Click "Create a room". A big 5-letter CODE appears.
  4. Click "Copy invite link" and send it to your friends in a text or chat.
     (Or tell them the web address and the code.)
  5. Watch your friends pop into the Players list.
  6. Pick your rules (or a preset), then click "Start game".

  IMPORTANT: Keep your browser page open the whole game. Your page is the
  referee. If you close it, the game stops for everyone. (If you accidentally
  close it, open the address again and click "Resume hosting".)

STEP 3 - JOIN A GAME (your friends)
  1. Open the invite link. The code is already filled in.
     (Or open the web address and type the code in the "Join" box.)
  2. Type a name, pick a token, click "Join".
  3. Wait for the host to start. That's it!

IF SOMEONE GETS DISCONNECTED
  - Their phone went to sleep or their Wi-Fi blinked? Have them open the
    game address again and click "Rejoin room". They get their player back
    with all their money and properties.
  - If they don't come back within about 20 seconds, a computer player plays
    for them so everyone else is not stuck waiting.

OTHER THINGS YOU CAN DO
  - "+ Computer player": adds a computer opponent to the room.
  - "+ Another player on this device": two people sharing the host's screen.
  - Keyboard shortcuts work for YOUR player: R roll, B buy, A auction,
    E end turn, J pay jail fine, C use jail card.

IF IT WON'T CONNECT
  - Make sure the host still has the game page open.
  - Double-check the code (it is 5 letters/numbers, no O, 0, I or 1).
  - Some Wi-Fi networks (work, school, hotels) block this kind of connection.
    Try mobile data or a different Wi-Fi.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - This uses a free public matchmaking service (PeerJS) to introduce your
    computers to each other. After that, game data goes straight between
    your browsers. It's free, but a free service can occasionally be slow.
  - Anyone who has your room code can join until the game starts. After the
    game starts, only the people already in it can come back.
  - Your original single-screen game (the "monopoly" folder) is not changed
    at all. You can still play it anytime.
