GAME NIGHT - HOW TO PLAY AND HOW TO UPDATE
===========================================

WHAT'S READY
  - Game menu with shared rooms (up to 8 people).
  - Snakes & Ladders (2-8 players).
  - Property Tycoon (2-4 players). For now it opens on its own page with its
    own room code. It moves into the shared room in a later stage.
  - The other games show "Coming soon".

START A GAME NIGHT (host)
  1. Open your game address. Type your name and pick a picture.
  2. Tap Snakes & Ladders. A 5-letter room code appears.
  3. Send friends the code, or tap "Copy invite link".
  4. Add computer players or "Another player on this device" if you like.
  5. Tap Start. KEEP THIS PAGE OPEN: your device runs the game.

JOIN (friends)
  Open the invite link (or the address + type the code), type a name, tap Join.

DURING THE NIGHT
  - Spin: tap SPIN! (keyboard: Space or R).
  - Emoji buttons send a reaction everyone can see.
  - Sound button at the top turns sound off or on (each device has its own).
  - After a game: "Play again" or "Choose a game". Everyone stays in the room
    and the scoreboard keeps counting wins.
  - More people than the game seats? Extra people watch. Use the up arrow in
    the player list to choose who plays.

LEAVING AND COMING BACK
  - Left by accident, or your screen went to sleep? Open the address and tap
    "Rejoin". You get your player back.
  - After about 20 seconds away, the computer plays for you until you return.
  - Different device? Type the code, then tap "That's me" next to your name.
    (This works once the computer is covering you or you show as Away.)
  - Anyone with the room code can join, so only share it with your group.

PAUSE AND FINISH LATER (host)
  Tap "Pause & save". Later, on the SAME device and browser, open the address
  and tap "Resume hosting". Friends tap "Rejoin". The scoreboard is saved too.

HOW TO UPDATE THE WEBSITE ON GITHUB
  1. On your computer open: Desktop > Claude > game-night
  2. Click inside the folder and press Ctrl+A to select every file.
  3. In your browser open:  https://github.com/Aneet87/monopoly/upload/main
  4. Drag the selected files onto that page. Wait until they are all listed.
  5. Click the green "Commit changes" button.
  6. Delete old files that are no longer used, if you see them: game.js, ui.js,
     style.css, HOW-TO-PLAY-ONLINE.txt, peerjs.min.js and relay-settings.js
     (Click the file name, then the "..." menu at the top right, then
     "Delete file", then "Commit changes".)
     Do NOT delete property-game.js, property-ui.js or property.css.
  7. Wait 2 minutes. Everyone refreshes the page (computer: Ctrl+Shift+R,
     phone: close the tab and open it again).

OPTIONAL: RENAME THE WEB ADDRESS
  Your address has "monopoly" in it. To change it: on the repository page click
  Settings, change "Repository name" to game-night, click Rename. Your new
  address becomes https://aneet87.github.io/game-night/ and the old link stops
  working, so send friends the new one. Then upload to
  https://github.com/Aneet87/game-night/upload/main next time.

THE CONNECTION SERVICE (GOOGLE FIREBASE)
  Moves travel through your free Firebase project "game-night". It works like an
  online whiteboard: one device writes a note, another reads it and wipes it off.
  Keep the project on the free "Spark" plan. Never add a payment card.

  One time only - publish the access rules (the lock):
    1. On your computer, right-click firebase-rules.json in the game-night
       folder, choose "Open with" > Notepad. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
    2. Go to console.firebase.google.com and open game-night.
    3. Open Realtime Database, then the "Rules" tab.
    4. Click inside the rules box, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V.
    5. Click "Publish".
  Also needed (one time): Security > Authentication > Sign-in method >
  Anonymous > Enable > Save.

IF IT WON'T CREATE OR JOIN A ROOM
  - Tap "Test my connection" on the first screen. It says what is wrong.
  - "Not fully set up": turn on Anonymous sign-in (above).
  - "Refused access": publish the rules (above).
  - "Could not reach the game service": check the internet, turn off any VPN or
    ad blocker for the game page, or try another network.
  - "Room was not found": check the code, and make sure the host has the game
    open on screen. A phone that goes to sleep pauses the host.

YOUR FILES
